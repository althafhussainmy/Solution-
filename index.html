College Timing — Right Time = Better Learning = Happier Students

What can better timing change?

Small improvements to the timetable can make the college day work better for students.

🚌

Easier commuting

Coordinate class start and finish times with public and college transport to reduce delays and long journeys.

📅

A balanced timetable

Reduce unnecessary gaps, provide a proper lunch break, and avoid classes running later than scheduled.

💬

Listen to students

Collect student feedback and include class representatives when reviewing schedule changes.

Let’s make it work for everyone.

Share this poster with classmates, student representatives, or college management to start a constructive conversation about college timings.
